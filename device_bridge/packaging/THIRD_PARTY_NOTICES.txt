Android MTP support uses dynamically linked libmtp and libusb.
Their license texts are included in licenses/libmtp/COPYING and licenses/libusb/COPYING.

libmtp 1.1.23 (unmodified): https://github.com/libmtp/libmtp/tree/v1.1.23
Source: https://github.com/libmtp/libmtp/archive/refs/tags/v1.1.23.tar.gz
libusb 1.0.30 (unmodified): https://github.com/libusb/libusb/tree/v1.0.30
Source: https://github.com/libusb/libusb/archive/refs/tags/v1.0.30.tar.gz

Native libraries are installed in _internal/native and may be replaced with
compatible versions. The bridge's macOS ad-hoc signature is not library validation.
Distributor: hsx_device_bridge. Native library authors retain their copyright.
